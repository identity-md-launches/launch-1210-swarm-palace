# Swarm Palace logo options

1. **logo-1.png — Crowned monarch:** an original lime frog face with a gold crown and violet cape; selected for `../artifacts/logo.png`.
2. **logo-2.png — Palace glyph:** a minimal gold palace silhouette whose towers also form frog eyes, on emerald.
3. **logo-3.png — SPALACE lettermark:** the exact ticker stacked as SPA / LACE in chunky lime and gold battlement lettering.
4. **logo-4.png — Royal mint:** a gold coin with an emerald center and an embossed crowned frog emblem.
5. **logo-5.png — Throne-room uproar:** a jubilant original frog king on a golden throne against a pink and violet comic burst.

All five are opaque 1024 × 1024 RGB PNGs, drawn with the built-in image generation tool (gpt-image); none were drawn with code. Five drafts were generated, one for each requested approach, with no discarded designs or redraws. The tool returned 1254 × 1254 originals; ffmpeg performed only Lanczos resizing to the required export dimensions. Redundant originals were removed to meet the 8 MiB source-bundle limit; all five exported designs and the selected logo remain byte-for-byte unchanged. No compositing or code-drawn elements were added to the logos.

The alternate approaches are retained as requested options, but not selected as the wallet mark: option 2 conveys less character; option 3 contains lettering; option 4 has a coin rim and fine metallic texture; option 5's hands, throne and burst are busier at 32 pixels. Option 1 gives the strongest simple frog-and-crown reading without text or a frame. The selected file is byte-for-byte identical to option 1.

`preview.html` displays every option at 64 pixels on light and dark backgrounds and the selected logo as a 32-pixel circle. Background color is baked into each PNG so the artwork retains its contrast on either surface. The alternate lettermark and coin badge intentionally follow their requested approaches; the no-text/no-frame wallet criteria apply to the selected mascot.
